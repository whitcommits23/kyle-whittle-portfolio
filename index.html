My Angle

I came up on the source side.

Most CX leaders come up through support and grow into the intelligence side. I came up the opposite direction.

I started at Qualtrics early, when the VoC category was still being defined. I learned how signals get collected, how programs get built, and what makes customer data trustworthy before I ever had to act on it.

Then a decade on the action side: content, enablement, onboarding. The teams that close the loop.

What I took from both sides: data alone doesn't move an organization. The teams receiving the insight have to feel equipped to act, and the win has to belong to them. That's shaped how I build.

Case Studies

What it looks like in practice.

Canopy Challenge

No structured onboarding ecosystem. New customers were left to figure out the product on their own, leading to early churn and overwhelmed support teams.

Approach

Designed and launched a connected training ecosystem: Knowledge Base, LMS, video library, and two certification programs, all built around a structured customer learning path.

Result

30% onboarding efficiency improvement • 40% churn reduction

Entrata Challenge

Zendesk knowledge base grown to 3,500+ articles across 60+ product lines with no governance, inconsistent taxonomy, and declining self-service rates.

Approach

Ran the Zendesk Copenhagen to Lotus migration, brought in AI-powered search, rebuilt taxonomy, applied KCS methodology, and stood up a Jira-driven content workflow with clear SLAs.

Result

45% support ticket deflection • 35% faster content turnaround

Qualtrics Challenge

Instructor-led training couldn't keep pace with customer growth across global markets. They needed an on-demand education model that could.

Approach

Built and launched Experience Basecamp on Skilljar LMS, digitizing 70+ training courses and creating a certification program customers could complete on demand.

Result

$1.6MM revenue generated • 100,000+ article views • 4,000+ likes
