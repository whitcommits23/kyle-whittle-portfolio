How I work

I organize chaos where customer signals, operations, and how people actually learn software all meet. For 10+ years I've built the systems that get customers to value faster and keep them there.

I write the code behind the dashboards. At Entrata I built a 4-agent AI pipeline in Python and GitHub that pulls patterns from customer data, surfaces where people get stuck, and routes the findings to close the gap before it compounds.

Entrata

Python, GitHub, Zendesk

AI pipelines for content work

Problem
A Zendesk knowledge base at 3,500+ articles across 60+ product lines. Manual updates ate the team's time.
Approach
Production pipelines built in Python and GitHub, with real version control behind them. Four specialized agents pull patterns from customer data, surface where people get stuck, and route the findings so the gap gets closed.
Result
Standard updates 62% faster, 8 hours down to 3, across 80% of the work. Complex drafting went from 14 hours to 90 minutes. That's 8+ hours per cycle back to the team.

Entrata

Zendesk, Jira, KCS

Knowledge and content ops

Problem
A large help center with no reliable way to tell which content was working, and SME bottlenecks slowing every release.
Approach
Ran the Zendesk Copenhagen to Lotus migration, rebuilt the taxonomy, applied KCS methodology and content signal scoring, and stood up a Jira content workflow with clear SLAs. Publishing now follows the release calendar.
Result
The library deflects 60% of support tickets traceable to documentation. Content turnaround is 35% faster, and self-service rose 25% year over year.

Onboarding and support

Problem
No structured onboarding. New customers were left to figure out the product on their own, which drove early churn and buried support.
Approach
Grew the onboarding org from 1 to 30 and built the stack from scratch: knowledge base, LMS, video library, and 2 certification programs. Put 900 to 1,000 customers a year through a repeatable program.
Result
Onboarding went from 3 to 4 months down to 30 to 60 days. Churn dropped 40%, and onboarding efficiency rose 30%.
