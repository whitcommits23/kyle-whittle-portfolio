My story

I organize chaos where customer signals, operations, and how people actually learn software all meet.

For 10+ years I've built the systems that get customers to value faster and keep them there.

At Qualtrics I worked across global markets helping enterprise teams build and read Voice of Customer programs. At Canopy I grew an implementation team from 1 to 30, cut onboarding from 3-4 months to 30-60 days, and put 900-1,000 customers a year through a repeatable program.

At Entrata I lead content operations across 60+ product lines and built an AI pipeline that pulls patterns from customer data, surfaces where people get stuck, and routes the findings to close the gap before it compounds. The library my team ran deflects 60% of support tickets traceable to documentation.

The approach is always the same: find where customers get stuck, trace it back to where it could have been prevented, fix it there, and build something that doesn't need reinventing next quarter.

I've worked in proptech, accounting, tax, and telecom. I know how to build from scratch and how to inherit a system that's barely holding together. Either way I can tell you what's wrong, what to do about it, and explain it to a VP or an IC depending on who's in the room.

What I believe

Work must be results-driven

Every program, article, or system I build is tied to a measurable outcome: adoption, retention, efficiency, revenue. If I can't define what success looks like, I go back to the problem statement.

The learner is always the customer

Whether I'm building for internal teams or external customers, the person who has to learn the thing is the one I design for. That means understanding their prior knowledge, their context, and their constraints, then building something they'll actually use.

Professionalism builds trust

I've worked with adults across market research, healthcare, automotive, financial, and manufacturing industries. That breadth taught me to be flexible, direct, and to respect people's time. The work I deliver reflects that: clear and built to last.
